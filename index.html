The Vision

Traditional microtonal lutherie is expensive, permanent, and difficult. MF3D changes the game by leveraging accessible 3D printing.

  • ✦
    Microtonal Made Easy: Slide-in fretboard mechanism for quick scale explorations without removing strings.
  • ✦
    Zero Damage: Easy installation, totally reversible. No glue or drilling required.
  • ✦
    Your Setup: Keep your current bridge and intonation mostly intact.
  • ✦
    Low Cost: Print it yourself DIY-style, or use third-party 3D printing services.

The Kit

Discover how the MF3D system seamlessly integrates with your instrument piece by piece.

01. The Foundation

The Base Piece

Attaches to the top of the neck and frets via double-sided tape. Features sliding slots for the fretboard.

02. The Heart

The Fretboard

Slides smoothly into the base piece. Easily hot-swappable. Supports print-in-place frets, modular slide-ins, or standard wire frets.

03. The Elevation

The Overnut

Goes over the current nut without unmounting it, elegantly elevating the strings to the new fretboard height.

04. The Compensation

Bridge Studs

Fit around current bridge screws to elevate the bridge, preserving intonation without replacing metal hardware.
